# Long Sword

## Combo Names

Overhead Combo = Overhead Slash > Overhead Slash > Overhead Slash

Spirit Blade Combo = Spirit Blade I > Spirit Blade II > Spirit Blade III > Spirit Roundslash

Crimson Slash Combo = Crimson Slash I > Crimson Slash II > Crimson Slash III

## Basic Moves:

### Fade Slash:

`(M1 + M2)` (Can be influenced with `WASD`)

## Simple Combos:

### Overhead Combo:

`M1 > M1 > M1`

### Thrust > Rising Slash

`M2 > M2`

### Thrust > (Overhead Combo > Rising Slash...):

`M2 > (M1 > M1 > M1 > M2 > ...)`

## Guage Fillers:

### Special Sheathe > Iai Slash > Rising Slash

`M2 > (M5 + Space) > M1 > M2`

## Guage Levelers:

### Spirit Blade Combo > Rising Slash:

`M5 > M5 > M5 > M5 > M2` (Requires meter)

### Fade Slash > Spirit Step Slash > Spirit Blade III > Spirit Roundslash > Thrust

`(M1 + M2) > M5 > M5 > M5 > M2`

## Counters:

### Foresight Slash > Spirit Roundslash:
`M2 > (M5 + M2) > M5` (Counter the hit with `(M5 + M2)`)

### Special Sheathe > Iai Spirit Slash
`M2 > (M5 + Space) > M5` (Counter the hit with `M5`)
